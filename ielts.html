FAQs – Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about the Express IELTS Academic practice test sets.

What is the Express IELTS Academic practice test set and where does it come from?
The Express IELTS Academic practice test set is compiled in the style of the official Cambridge book series. Each set contains multiple tests covering Listening, Reading and Writing, reflecting actual past exam difficulty and question styles. It is a trusted, closely aligned source widely recommended by the IELTS community for effective practice.
Which skills are included in the Express IELTS Academic test set?
Each set includes three skills: Listening (30 min, 40 questions), Reading (60 min, 40 questions) and Writing (60 min, Task 1 + Task 2). Full Tests combine all three in one sitting, just like the real computer-based exam.
Why can't I find the IELTS Speaking practice in the test library?
Speaking is assessed face-to-face and cannot be part of a written test set. That's why we built a dedicated AI Speaking Room — an AI examiner asks Parts 1–3 questions and scores you on all four criteria. Try the AI Speaking Room →
Is the difficulty of the Express Academic test set equivalent to the real exam?
Yes. The sets are authored to match the official exam's difficulty band and question-type distribution, so your mock scores are a reliable indicator of your real performance level (±0.5 band).
What is the difference between IELTS Academic and General Training test sets?
Academic is for university admission — Reading passages are academic in nature and Writing Task 1 is a graph/chart description. General Training is for migration/work — Reading uses everyday texts and Task 1 is a letter. The Foreign Express currently offers Academic sets; General Training is coming soon.
